Design Guidelines

  • Minimum size: 48×48 pixels for interface icons
  • Maintain padding/spacing within 16-20% of the logo size
  • Works on dark and light backgrounds
  • Scales well from favicon (16px) to large formats (512px+)
  • Export as SVG for web (scalable), PNG for raster use
  • Ensure 1:1 aspect ratio for consistency
